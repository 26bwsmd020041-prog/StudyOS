📖 How to Configure Your AI Tutor (3 Easy Steps)

  1. Step 1 — Choose an Provider Endpoint: Get an OpenAI-compatible URL from your backend or provider.
    • OpenAI Direct: https://api.openai.com/v1/chat/completions
    • Groq (Free/Fast): https://api.groq.com/openai/v1/chat/completions
    • Custom Server/Proxy: https://your-domain.com/api/chat
  2. Step 2 — Get Your API Key: Log in to your provider account (e.g., OpenAI or Groq) and create an API Key.
  3. Step 3 — Save & Ask: Paste the endpoint and key into the fields below, click "Save AI settings", and start asking questions!
